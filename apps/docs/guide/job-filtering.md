# LinkedIn Smart Job Filtering

LinkedIn job feeds are filled with promoted ad listings, reposted ghost positions, and listings with hundreds of applicants. OpenApply provides intelligent real-time filtering directly inside your LinkedIn search feed.

---

## 🎯 Filtering Features

### 1. Promoted Listing Dimming
Sponsored listings often re-appear repeatedly and yield lower response rates. OpenApply automatically applies a subtle opacity filter to sponsored cards so you can focus on organic postings.

### 2. Applicant Threshold Sliders
Set your preferred maximum applicant threshold in the OpenApply extension popup (e.g., **50 applicants**).
- Job cards with fewer than 50 applicants remain highlighted.
- Job cards exceeding 50 applicants are visually dimmed with an inline warning badge (*"Over 50 applicants"*).

### 3. Deceptive "Fake Remote" Warnings
Many jobs are listed as "Remote" in the title, but include hidden restrictions in the fine print. OpenApply scans descriptions for phrases like:
- *"Must relocate within 30 days"*
- *"Hybrid schedule: 3 days in office required"*
- *"Must reside in specific state for tax purposes"*

Cards containing these clauses display a **⚠️ Fake Remote Warning** badge with clickable citation reasons.
