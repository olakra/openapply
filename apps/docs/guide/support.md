# Support & Sponsorship

OpenApply is **100% free, open-source software** built under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

We believe job seekers should not have to pay expensive monthly subscriptions or compromise their privacy just to land a job or keep their state unemployment benefits compliant.

---

## ❤️ Ways to Support OpenApply

If OpenApply helped you land an interview, saved you time, or gave you peace of mind during your job search, please consider supporting the project:

### 1. Sponsor the Core Maintainers
Your financial support pays for open-source maintenance, domain hosting, and extension store developer accounts.

- ☕ **Buy Me a Coffee**: [![Buy Me A Coffee](https://img.shields.io/badge/Buy%20Me%20A%20Coffee-Donate-yellow.svg?logo=buy-me-a-coffee)](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/olakra)
- ❤️ **Patreon**: [patreon.com/olakra](https://www.patreon.com/olakra)
- 💖 **GitHub Sponsors**: [github.com/sponsors/olakra](https://github.com/sponsors/olakra)

### 2. Star & Share the Project
- ⭐ **Star on GitHub**: Give our repository a star on [GitHub (olakra/openapply)](https://github.com/olakra/openapply).
- 📣 **Share with Job Seekers**: Share OpenApply with colleagues, career transition groups, or on LinkedIn/X!

### 3. Contribute Code or Prompts
We welcome pull requests! Whether it's adding new LLM strategies, fixing bugs, or improving prompt templates:
- Check out open issues on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/olakra/openapply/issues).
- Read our `AGENTS.md` and repository guidelines before submitting PRs.
