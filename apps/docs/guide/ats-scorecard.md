# 1-Click ATS Scorecards

OpenApply evaluates job descriptions against candidate resume skills to provide an instant ATS fit score (0-100%).

---

## 📊 Scorecard Metrics

When you click **"Score Position"** on any LinkedIn job listing, OpenApply generates a comprehensive breakdown:

- **Overall Fit Score**: Calculated weighted percentage match.
- **Key Matching Skills**: Green badges highlighting exact skill overlaps (e.g. `TypeScript`, `React`, `GraphQL`).
- **Critical Missing Skills**: Red badges calling out required keywords missing from your resume profile.
- **Executive Summary**: 2-sentence summary providing actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎭 Evaluation Personas

You can toggle the evaluation persona in the prompt config:

1. **Technical Recruiter**: Prioritizes keyword density, years of experience, and certification matches.
2. **Engineering Manager**: Focuses on problem-solving impact, system architecture capabilities, and team execution.
3. **Tech Lead / Architect**: Scrutinizes exact technology stacks and software design patterns.
4. **VP of Engineering**: Evaluates strategic alignment, leadership, and high-level team acceleration.
