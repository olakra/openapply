# AI Cover Letter Generator

OpenApply generates concise, 3-paragraph tailored cover letters designed to grab recruiter attention without generic filler phrases.

---

## 📝 3-Paragraph Composition Structure

1. **Paragraph 1 (The Hook)**: Direct, high-impact opening statement matching your background to the company's core mission.
2. **Paragraph 2 (Proven Evidence)**: Specific metrics and work history achievements proving your ability to solve the core requirements in the job description.
3. **Paragraph 3 (Call to Action)**: Professional close inviting a follow-up discussion.

---

## ⚡ Customization & Tone Guidance

You can provide custom instructions in your Candidate Profile settings:

- _"Emphasize my experience scaling distributed backend microservices."_
- _"Keep tone energetic, concise, and focused on startup growth metrics."_

The cover letter generator incorporates your guidance while adhering strictly to maximum 250-word limits.
